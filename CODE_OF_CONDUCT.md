# Code of Conduct

## Welcome

This project is a safe space to build, share and learn games. Rather than experience, it does not matter. Everyone is welcome.

## Be Respectful

- Be kind and respectful to all.
- Don't worry about people who are new to the game.
- Give helpful feedback.
- Accept conflicting views and ideas.
- Do not insult, bully or harass anyone.

If an error is made, explain the problem. Do not tease him/her.

## Reporting problems

If you see or experience bad behavior, contact on GitHub: [sabirhusseinbalal](https://github.com/sabirhusseinbalal). You can open an issue, or message me if you want it kept private.

I may delete comments or code, reject contributions, or block people who don't follow these rules.

## Final note

We're here to learn and have fun making games together.

Be kind. Be helpful. Keep building.
